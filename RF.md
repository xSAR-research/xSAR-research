# xSAR RF

[← Back to the xSAR repository map](README.md)

**Status:** Early development

Radio-frequency sensing, timing, positioning and data links across LORAN, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth systems.

Coverage includes:

- LORAN timing and positioning research;
- Wi-Fi link measurement and Linux integration;
- Bluetooth and Bluetooth Low Energy device communications;
- antenna, interference and signal-quality observations;
- timestamping, synchronisation and range-related measurements;
- repeatable RF test conditions and captured evidence.
